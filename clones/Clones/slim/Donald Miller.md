# Donald Miller — Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Donald Miller.md

## StoryBrand SB7 Framework

7 steps to clear brand messaging:

1. **Character (Hero/Client)** — Define what the client WANTS (specific desire)
2. **Problem** — 3 levels: External (practical), Internal (feeling), Philosophical (injustice)
3. **Guide (Your Brand)** — Show Empathy ("I understand") + Authority ("I know how to fix it")
4. **Plan** — Simple 3-step plan. Max 4. Client must know EXACTLY what to do
5. **Call to Action** — Direct CTA ("Buy now") + Transitional CTA ("Download free guide")
6. **Failure** — What the client LOSES if they don't act. Stakes create urgency
7. **Success** — Clear transformation. Before vs. After

## The Grunt Test

3 questions answered in 5 seconds or you failed:
- What do you offer?
- How does it improve my life?
- What do I need to do to buy?

## Momentos Espelho (Audience Sees Themselves)

- The customer is the HERO, never the brand
- The internal problem (frustration, shame, fear) matters more than the external one
- Position the brand as the GUIDE (Yoda, not Luke)
- "Your customer is [Luke Skywalker]. You are [Yoda]."

## Key Patterns

- "If you confuse, you'll lose."
- "People don't buy the best products. They buy the products they can understand the fastest."
- "The customer is the hero, not your brand."
- "Clarify your message. Grow your business."
- Even 15 steps must be grouped into 3 macro-steps
