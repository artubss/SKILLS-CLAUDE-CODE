# MrBeast -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/MrBeast.md

## Retention Architecture

- **Open Loop Stacking:** Never let tension drop to zero. When one mini-tension resolves, immediately introduce the next. The video is a chain of dopamine loops.
- **Escalation of Stakes:** Each segment must raise the stakes vs the previous one. Exponential, not linear.
- **Payoff Engineering:** The viewer must feel the video was worth it. Deliver on the title's promise -- broken promise = channel death.
- **Crazy Progression:** Cover multiple days/events in the first 3 minutes. Get the viewer deeply invested fast instead of spending 3 min on Day 1.
- **3-Minute Re-engagement:** At minute 3, insert something "only MrBeast could do" -- a segment that genuinely impresses.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** "Stop telling people what they will be watching and start showing them."

## Second-by-Second Retention Machine

- 0-1s: Pattern interrupt visual (absurd image, action, explosion)
- 1-5s: Verbal HOOK -- video premise in 1 sentence
- 5-30s: VALIDATE the thumbnail promise (show it)
- 30-60s: Emotional INVESTMENT -- viewer meets characters/stakes
- 1-3 min: Crazy progression -- cover multiple events rapidly
- 3 min: RE-ENGAGEMENT -- high-impact moment
- 3-6 min: Most exciting content of the entire video
- 6+ min: Progressive ESCALATION to climax
- Final: Satisfying PAYOFF

## Thumbnail & Title Rules

- **1 focal point** -- single thing to look at, minimal cognitive load
- **Vibrant colors** -- high contrast against YouTube's white background
- **Expressive face** -- clear emotion (shock, surprise, excitement)
- **Max 3-4 words** on thumbnail, bold oversized font
- **Must work on mobile** -- if it fails small, it fails
- **Title: monosyllables** -- short words reduce reading effort
- **Active voice** -- "I Survived" not "Survival Was Attempted"
- **6th grade reading level** -- universally understandable
- **No empty clickbait** -- title MUST be delivered in the video

## The Sacred Triad (Metrics)

- **CTR** (thumbnail + title) -- target ~13% vs industry ~4%
- **AVD** (hook + structure + pacing) -- target ~13min37s
- **AVP** (consistent retention + escalation) -- target 50%+

## Key Patterns & Catchphrases

- "I [extreme verb] [absurd premise]!" (hook formula)
- "Let's go!" (universal action catchphrase)
- "But wait, it gets crazier." (escalation transition)
- "We're not done yet." (signals next level)
- "Does physics allow it? Then yes, it's possible."
- "Your first 100 videos are going to suck. Just accept it."
- "I've reinvested everything to the point of stupidity."
- "For 50 million people to understand something, it must be simple."
