## LinkedIn Algorithm Data (2025, 1.8M posts analyzed)

### Reach Multipliers by Format

| Format | Reach Multiplier | Engagement Rate | Notes |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|-------|
| Poll | 1.64x | High | 3 options, 7 days. Only 1.4% of posts. |
| Document/Carousel | 1.45x | 6.60% (highest) | 8-10 slides sweet spot. |
| Image | 1.18x | Medium | Photos of people = +50% engagement. |
| Video | 1.10x | Medium-high | Short-form vertical <60s. Usage +69% YoY. |
| Text-only | 1.00x (baseline) | - | 1800-2100 chars ideal. Company pages: 0.28x. |
| External Link | ~0.65-0.75x | Low | Improved in 2025. Copy <300 words helps. |

### Macro Trends 2025

- Organic reach down 50% YoY -- each impression worth more
- Engagement down 25% YoY -- genuine comments are rarer and more valuable
- Follower growth down 59% -- patience is key
- Algorithm prioritizes topical interest -- niche focus rewarded, generalists penalized
- Conversation-focused: comments > likes > shares

## Social Signal Hierarchy

| Signal | Weight | Effect |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Save (bookmark) | 5-10x a like | Super-signal. Content worth revisiting. |
| Repost (instant) | Very high | Distribution to reposter's network. |
| Comment >15 words | 2x short comment | Deep conversation signals quality. |
| DM after post | 90% see next post | Strong relationship signal. |
| Profile visit | 60% visibility boost | Deep interest signal. |
| Comment on someone | 80% see their next | Algorithmic reciprocity. |
| Like | Low | Vanity metric, lowest weight. |

## Engagement Optimization Rules

- **Golden Hour:** Reply to ALL comments in first 45-60 min. Add 2-4 own comments after 1h = +25% growth.
- **Don't Post & Ghost:** Active nurturing for 24-48h after publishing.
- **Pre-publish:** Engage on 5+ posts from your inner circle 15 min before posting.
- **Consistency > Frequency:** 3x/week for months > sporadic 7x/week bursts.
- **Hashtags:** Max 3 (or zero -- near-zero impact in 2025, pages deactivated Oct 2024).
- **External links:** Publish without link, edit to add later. Or place in comments.
- **Text formatting:** Max 4 lines per paragraph = +15% reach. Mobile-first (72% traffic).

## Format Recommendations: When to Use Each

- **Carousel/Document:** Educational content, data insights, checklists. Highest engagement rate.
- **Text-only:** Storytelling, personal/vulnerable posts. 1800-2100 chars sweet spot.
- **Image:** Personal photos (selfie = +50% engagement). Good for brand building.
- **Video:** Short-form vertical <60s. Growing fast (+69% usage YoY).
- **Poll:** High reach but use sparingly. 3 options, 7-day duration.
- **Newsletter:** For owned audience. Consistent cadence.

## 8 Ingredients of the Perfect Post

1. **Hook** -- first line stops the scroll
2. **Format** -- matched to objective
3. **Copy length** -- 700-900 chars with visual, 1800-2100 text-only
4. **Formatting** -- short paragraphs, lists, white space (+15% reach)
5. **Visual** -- person photo or infographic
6. **CTA** -- Call-to-Think or Call-to-Feel > direct Call-to-Action
7. **Hashtags** -- max 3 or zero
8. **PS** -- closing question to generate comments
