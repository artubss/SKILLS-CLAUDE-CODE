# Ryan Holiday -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Ryan Holiday.md

## Perennial Seller Framework

Create content that lasts decades, not days. 4 pillars:

- **Creation:** Demands time, iterations, and a ruthless editor. "Nobody ever regrets making something too good."
- **Positioning:** Know FOR WHOM and WHY. Focus on audience needs, not yours.
- **Marketing:** Avoid obvious paths. Build sustainable organic growth. "The better your product is, the better your marketing will be."
- **Platform:** Email list, community, relationships -- tools that work repeatedly over a career.

**Master principle:** "The key to success for many perennial sellers is that their creators don't distinguish between the making and the marketing."

## Stoic Principles for Timeless Content

**Three Disciplines (The Obstacle Is The Way):**
- **Perception:** See events as neutral, not good or bad. Clarity before action.
- **Action:** Directed, intentional -- never reactive. Dismantles obstacles.
- **Will:** Inner strength for what you can't control.

**Four Virtues:** Courage > Discipline > Justice > Wisdom. Every ethical/strategic problem filters through these.

## What Makes Content Last Beyond the News Cycle

- **Universalize the lesson:** Strip away the specific event, find the timeless principle underneath
- **Density of principle:** Each sentence must carry information or insight. Zero filler.
- **The Perennial Test:** Would this make sense if read 5 years from now? If it depends on a trend, it's not lasting.
- **Historical anchoring:** Connect to examples from different centuries -- if the same principle held then and now, it's universal
- **One book rule:** Content must be either extremely entertaining OR extremely practical. "Most fail because they fall somewhere in between."
- **Anti-hype:** Replace sensationalism with gravity. Restraint is strength. Silence is confidence.

## Trading Up The Chain (Media Strategy)

Small blog > Medium site > Large outlet > Mainstream media. Each level validates the previous. Use earned coverage as proof for the next tier.
