## STEPPS Framework (Contagious)

Why things go viral -- 6 principles for word of mouth:

- **Social Currency:** People share what makes them look good. Use inner remarkability, game mechanics, scarcity/exclusivity.
- **Triggers:** Environmental cues keep ideas top of mind. Frequency of trigger > strength of trigger. (Kit Kat + coffee = +33% sales)
- **Emotion:** High-arousal emotions drive sharing (awe, excitement, anger, anxiety). Low-arousal kills it (sadness, contentment). Arousal matters more than positive/negative.
- **Public (Observability):** What's visible is imitable. "If something is built to show, it's built to grow." Self-advertising products + behavioral residue.
- **Practical Value:** Useful info gets shared. "News you can use." Rule of 100: below $100 use %, above $100 use absolute discount.
- **Stories:** Narratives are Trojan Horses -- info rides along inside stories. The story must be integral to the message.

## REDUCE Framework (The Catalyst)

Remove barriers instead of pushing harder. "Sometimes change doesn't require more horsepower -- just unlock the parking brake."

- **Reactance:** Pressure creates resistance. Offer a menu, ask don't tell, highlight gaps.
- **Endowment:** People overvalue status quo. Surface the cost of inaction, harness loss aversion.
- **Distance:** Ideas too far from current beliefs get rejected. Find the movable middle, ask for less first.
- **Uncertainty:** Change is risky. Use freemium, reduce upfront costs, make it reversible.
- **Corroborating Evidence:** Some changes need proof from multiple sources. Concentrate and diversify sources.

## Contagion Triggers & Share Mechanics

- Only 7% of word of mouth happens online -- majority is face-to-face
- WoM drives 20-50% of all purchasing decisions
- Emotional volatility (ups and downs) in a narrative engages more than constant emotion
- Uncertainty emotions (anxiety, hope, surprise) hold attention longer than certain emotions (sadness)
- Identity language > action language: "Don't be a cheater" reduces cheating 50% more than "Don't cheat"

## SPEACC Framework (Magic Words)

- **Similarity:** Match audience language for trust; differ for attention
- **Posing Questions:** Follow-up questions show interest. "What problems does it have?" > "Anything I should know?"
- **Emotion:** Emotional language = +30% read-through. Use emotional volatility, not monotone.
- **Agency/Identity:** Nouns > verbs for behavior change. "Be a helper" > "Please help."
- **Confidence:** Ditch hedges (maybe, sort of). Use present tense. Use definites (definitely, clearly).
- **Concreteness:** Specific details activate senses and show you listened.
