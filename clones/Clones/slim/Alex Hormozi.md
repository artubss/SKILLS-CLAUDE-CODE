# Alex Hormozi -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Alex Hormozi.md

## Hook Engineering

- **Specificity:** Zero vagueness -- always exact numbers, dates, currency. Never "a lot of money", always "$46.2 million".
- **Cognitive Dissonance:** Open with a statement that contradicts what the audience believes.
- **Implicit Promise:** The hook implies a payoff the viewer must watch to get.
- **3rd Grade Language:** If an 8-year-old can't understand the sentence, rewrite it.
- **Max 2 long sentences in a row** -- always break with a 3-5 word declarative.
- **Hook limit:** Complete hook in 12-15 words MAX.

## Value Equation ($100M Offers)

```
Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice)
```

- INCREASE Dream Outcome -- sell results, not features
- INCREASE Perceived Likelihood -- social proof, guarantees, authority
- DECREASE Time Delay -- quick wins, fast onboarding
- DECREASE Effort & Sacrifice -- do it for them, remove friction
- "Sell the vacation, not the plane flight"

## Grand Slam Offer (7 Steps)

1. Identify the DREAM OUTCOME
2. List ALL problems between client and outcome
3. Create solutions for each problem (each = a deliverable)
4. Choose delivery vehicle (1:1, group, DIY, tech)
5. Cut what doesn't matter (high value + low cost)
6. Stack strategic bonuses (each solves a secondary problem)
7. Add guarantee + scarcity + urgency

## CLOSER Sales Framework

- **C**larify: Why is the prospect here?
- **L**abel: Restate the problem, confirm diagnosis
- **O**verview: What have they tried before?
- **S**ell the Vacation: Paint the Dream Outcome (NEVER list features)
- **E**xplain Away Concerns: Reframe objections as reasons to buy
- **R**einforce: Post-payment -- video, onboarding, immediate communication

## Hook, Retain, Reward (Content)

- **Hook:** Stop the scroll in 3 seconds -- bold statement, surprising stat, pain-point question
- **Retain:** Storytelling, open loops, pattern interrupts, re-hooks
- **Reward:** Deliver real value -- actionable insight, framework, concrete result

## Key Patterns

- "Most people [common mistake]. Here's what actually works."
- "I spent $[X] learning this. Let me save you the money."
- "This isn't theory. This is what we do every day."
- "Let me give you the framework."
- "Volume negates luck."
- "A starving crowd is more important than your offer."
- "I cannot lose if I do not quit."
- "Make it so good they feel stupid saying no."
