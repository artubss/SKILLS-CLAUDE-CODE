## APAG Framework (Persuasion)
- **A (Attention):** Open a curiosity gap -- make the reader NEED to know more
- **P (Perspective):** Change how the reader sees the problem -- reframe
- **A (Advantage):** Show the clear benefit of adopting the new perspective
- **G (Gamify):** Structure the solution as progress -- numbered steps, checkpoints

## Introduce > Educate > Simplify
- **Introduce (1/3):** Present the problem. Open the curiosity loop.
- **Educate (1/3):** Explain WHY this problem exists. Context, consequences, philosophy.
- **Simplify (1/3):** Show HOW to solve it. Clear framework, practical steps, examples.

## One-Person Business Model
1. **Foundation:** Writing (the core skill that feeds EVERYTHING)
2. **Distribution:** Social media (tweets, posts, videos -- all starts as text)
3. **Deepening:** Newsletter (direct relationship, no algorithm)
4. **Monetization:** Digital product (course, community, software)
5. **Scale:** Content works 24/7, no large team needed
- **Flywheel:** 1 idea > tweet > thread > newsletter > video > course

## The Anti-Niche
- Your niche is NOT a topic. Your niche is YOU.
- The unique intersection of your skills + experiences + curiosities = positioning impossible to copy.
- The more you try to niche into 1 topic, the more generic you become.

## Core Principles
- Writing is the meta-skill of the digital age
- Philosophy first, tactics second -- every tactic needs a deep "why"
- 4 hours of deep focus > 12 hours of scattered grind
- "The problem isn't [X]. The problem is [Y]." -- always reframe
- Education-based content sells more than entertainment
- Depth > volume. The internet needs more thinking, not more content.
