## Misconception-Led Learning

**Core finding (PhD Thesis, 2008):**
Videos that start with the WRONG belief then contradict it produce +42% retention vs. clear direct explanations. Confusion is not a bug — it is the feature.

**Why it works:** When the viewer agrees with info they already believe, the brain marks it as confirmed and doesn't store it. When info CONTRADICTS their belief, the brain creates an "error to correct" tag — demanding active attention and consolidating memory.

## The 3-Step Misconception Loop

1. **Activate the Wrong Belief** — Present the misconception the MAJORITY holds. Let the viewer internally agree.
   - Must be genuinely what most people believe (not a straw man)
2. **Create Cognitive Dissonance** — Present the fact that directly contradicts the activated belief. The viewer feels "wait, that can't be right."
3. **Resolve with Insight** — Explain WHY the misconception exists and what reality is. Resolution is both intellectually satisfying and emotionally relieving.

## 5 Types of Misconceptions

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Cause/Effect Inversion | Audience believes A caused B; reality is B caused A | "Nike's success came from marketing" (actually product came first) |
| Correlation/Cause Confusion | Two facts seem connected but aren't | Blockbuster didn't fail because of streaming |
| Scale Flip | Underestimate or overestimate impact | "R$50M seems like a lot. It was 0.2% of revenue." |
| Intention vs. Result | Assumes intention equals outcome | Patagonia asked people NOT to buy — sold 40% more |
| Mistaken Identity | Believes they know what something IS | "Starbucks sells coffee" (actually sells workspace) |

## Prediction Error Principle

- Brain works as a prediction machine
- Confirmed prediction = minimal dopamine
- VIOLATED prediction = MAXIMUM dopamine spike = attention
- Rule: violation every 3-5 seconds (visual, verbal, or emotional level)

## Key Patterns

- "What does the majority BELIEVE about this topic that isn't true?" (mandatory question before any script)
- The misconception must appear in the first 10 seconds
- "Confusion is not a bug in the learning process. It is the feature."
