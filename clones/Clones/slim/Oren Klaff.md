# Oren Klaff — Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Oren Klaff.md

## Frame Control

When two frames collide, only one survives. The dominant frame controls who has power, status, and authority.

```
Weak frame:  "Please stay. I promise it gets good."
Strong frame: "I have something few know. If you leave, you lose."
```

## The 4 Frames of Attention

**Sequence for maximum retention:** Power --> Intrigue --> Prize --> Prize+Time

1. **Power Frame** — Authority via specificity. Precise data = immediate credibility.
   - Weak: "I think this strategy might work..."
   - Strong: "This strategy generated R$2B. Let me show you why."

2. **Intrigue Frame** — Story with unresolved tension. Viewer stays because NOT knowing the ending is psychologically disturbing.
   - "Something happened with [company]... and what I discovered changed everything."

3. **Prize Frame** — The creator is the prize. Viewer must EARN the information.
   - Weak: "In this video I'll teach you..."
   - Strong: "Few people understand this. Those who do will..."

4. **Time Frame** — Calibrated urgency (not fake). "This is happening now. In 3 years everyone will know. But today, only those who watch until the end understand."

## Crocodile Brain Test

Decisions are made by the primitive brain (amygdala) in 1.7 seconds BEFORE reaching the prefrontal cortex. The croc brain asks only 3 questions:

1. Is this a threat? (If boring/corporate = threat = leave)
2. Is this new/unexpected? (If no = leave)
3. Is this relevant to my survival/status? (If no = leave)

**Rule:** The hook must activate all 3 positive filters in 1.7 seconds.

## Flash Roll (Status in 3 Seconds)

```
[1 concrete result data] + [1 specificity data] + [1 status provocation]
```

Example: "The company that dominated 60% of the market in 18 months had no product, no team, no money. What it had was a frame."

## Frame Stacking for Content

```
[0-5s]    POWER FRAME: implicit credential via specificity
[5-30s]   INTRIGUE FRAME: story with unresolved tension
[30-60s]  PRIZE FRAME: viewer is "earning" exclusive knowledge
[60-90s]  TIME FRAME: "this is happening now"
[90-120s] POWER FRAME: status confirmation via lesson
[120-150s] PRIZE + TIME: CTA as exclusive access
```

## Key Patterns

- "When two frames collide, the stronger frame absorbs the weaker frame."
- "Attention is not given. It is won. And it is won by whoever controls the frame."
- Never use: "I'll show you", "Today I'll talk about" (frame-ceding language)
- Replace with abundance frames: "Those who understand this will..."
