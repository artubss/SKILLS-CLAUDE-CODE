# Justin Welsh -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Justin Welsh.md

## LinkedIn Operating System (LOS)
1. **Profile Optimization:** Headline as value proposition, About as landing page, Featured as showcase
2. **Content Engine:** Post 1x/day, hook above the fold, visual (carousel/image), soft CTA
3. **Engagement System:** 30 min/day commenting on strategic posts (bigger creators)
4. **Monetization Funnel:** Free content > newsletter > digital product > 1:1 premium

## Content Operating System (COS)
- 1 weekly newsletter (1000-1200 words) = central piece
- Newsletter becomes 6-12 short posts (LinkedIn, X)
- Each short post can become carousel, thread, or video
- Ideation in batch: 30 min, 2x/week = 10-14 ideas

## Content Matrix (Ideation Engine)
- Cross topics with formats to generate ideas instantly
- **Topics:** Your 3-5 areas of expertise
- **Formats:** How-to, Mistakes, Before/After, Frameworks, Listicles, Contrarian Takes, Stories
- 5 topics x 7 formats = 35 instant ideas

## Scroll-Stopping Hooks
- **PAIPS:** Pain > Agitate > Intrigue > Positive Future > Solution
- **Number-Result-Period:** "I grew from [X] to [Y] in [Z] months. Here's the system:"
- **Temporal Contrast:** "[X] years ago: [bad]. Today: [good]. The [N] things that changed:"
- Hook must be above the fold. If line 1 doesn't stop the scroll, rewrite.

## Content Pillar Alignment
- 1 post = 1 idea. Never mix topics.
- Max 2 lines per paragraph on LinkedIn.
- 4 value posts for every 1 sell post (4:1 ratio).
- Everything from personal experience -- never generic.
- Systems thinking: "You don't need more content. You need a system."

## Post Structure
1. Provocative statement (grab attention)
2. Personal context (credibility via experience)
3. Numbered framework (clear, scannable)
4. Concrete example (proof it works)
5. Measurable result (number, metric, timeline)
6. Soft CTA (invitation, not command)

## Key Patterns
- "I built a $5M+ solo business. Here's the playbook."
- "Stop posting. Start systemizing."
- "It's not about [X]. It's about [Y]."
- "This isn't theory. This is my daily system."
- "Bookmark this. You'll need it."
- "Most [group] think they need [X]. They don't. They need [Y]."
- "Found this helpful? Repost to share with your network."
- "It's simple, not easy."
