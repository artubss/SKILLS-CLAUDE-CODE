## Demand Gen Triad: Create / Capture / Convert

- **Create Demand:** Before buyer is in-market. Channels: dark social, podcasts, communities, LinkedIn organic. NOT measurable by attribution software. Measure with Self-Reported Attribution (SRA).
- **Capture Demand:** When buyer is actively searching. Channels: Google Ads, G2, Capterra, brand search. Measurable by attribution software.
- **Convert Demand:** After buyer reaches out. Inbound sales, demo, proposal. Buyer already decided -- close quickly.
- **Key insight:** Most companies spend 80%+ on Capture. Winners invest heavily in Creation, where 60-70% of pipeline actually originates (per SRA data).

## Dark Social / Dark Funnel

- Dark Social = sharing via untraceable channels (WhatsApp, Slack, DMs, word of mouth, podcasts)
- Dark Funnel = buyer decision touchpoints marketing can't measure
- 75%+ of path-to-purchase happens in the dark funnel
- Buyers are 70-80% decided before talking to sales
- Attribution software under-reports social impact by 75%+

## Self-Reported Attribution (SRA) vs Software

- Software shows: 82% Google/Direct, 10% Social, 8% Other
- SRA shows: 44% Social, 30% Podcast, 13% Community, 10% WoM
- Only 7% of SQOs matched between software and self-reported data
- Implementation: free-text "How did you hear about us?" field -- NEVER use helper text

## Anti-MQL / Modern Revenue Metrics

- MQLs don't correlate with revenue -- replace with: Revenue, Qualified Pipeline, CAC, Sales Velocity, CAC Payback
- Inbound has 50-67% lower CAC than outbound
- HIRO Pipeline (High Intent Revenue Opportunities): leads from high-intent sources with >3% win rate
