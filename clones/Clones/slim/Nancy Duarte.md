# Nancy Duarte — Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Nancy Duarte.md

## Sparkline Framework

Oscillate repeatedly between two states to create tension:
- **What Is** — Current reality, problems, limitations, pain
- **What Could Be** — Vision, possibilities, transformed future
- Repeat 5-7 oscillations (not just 1 round trip)
- **Climax** — Strongest contrast point (STAR Moment)
- **The New Bliss** — End with the transformed vision, call to adventure

Why it works: the brain responds to CONTRAST. No contrast = no tension = no attention.

## Slide:ology (Visual Composition)

- **Dado Heroi = 70% of space** — The hero data point gets dominant visual weight
- Signal-to-noise ratio: every visual must serve the narrative
- Less slides, more impact — each slide serves the story

## DataStory (Data + Narrative)

3-act structure for data:
1. **Setup** — Context: what we're looking at and why it matters
2. **Conflict** — What the data reveals that is surprising or concerning
3. **Resolution** — Actionable recommendation based on the data

Rule: Data without story is noise. Story without data is opinion.

## S.T.A.R. Moment (Something They'll Always Remember)

5 types: Memorable dramatization, Repeatable sound bite, Evocative visual, Emotional story, Shocking statistic. Every presentation needs at least 1.

## The New Bliss

The destination — the transformed vision the audience embraces. Always end here: "Imagine a world where..."

## Key Patterns

- "What is... vs. What could be."
- "The audience is the hero, not the presenter."
- "Data without story is just noise."
- "Contrast creates interest. Contrast creates movement."
- "Your Big Idea must be stated in one complete sentence."
