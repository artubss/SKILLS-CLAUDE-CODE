# Gary Vaynerchuk -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Gary Vaynerchuk.md

## Day Trading Attention (DTA)

Attention is the most valuable currency. "Day-trade" it by finding where it's UNDERPRICED.

6 pillars:
- **Cohorts:** Identify specific audience segments -- never talk to "everyone"
- **PAC (Platform and Culture):** Create content native to each platform's psychology AND current cultural moment. LinkedIn != TikTok != Instagram.
- **SOC (Strategic Organic Content):** Authenticity with intention -- not random posting, but content designed to resonate organically
- **Amplification:** Use paid media ONLY to amplify what already proved organically. Don't boost everything.
- **Creative:** "Creative is the variable of success." Not budget, not brand -- the CREATIVE determines performance.
- **Post-Creative Strategy (PCS):** Read comments, use post-publish data to improve next content.

**Interest Graph vs Social Graph:** All platforms shifting to TikTok model -- distribution by interest, not followers. Good content > big following.

## Jab Jab Jab Right Hook (JJJRH)

- **Jab:** Content that delivers value, emotion, entertainment -- asking NOTHING in return
- **Right Hook:** The ask. CTA. Buy, subscribe, download.
- Value ALWAYS comes before the ask. Ratio isn't rigid 3:1, but value must create sufficient goodwill first.
- Each jab must be NATIVE to its platform
- The jab isn't weak content waiting for the right hook -- it's STRONG content that stands alone

## Platform Authenticity Rules

- NEVER post the same content across all platforms -- adapt format, language, tone
- "Respect the platform" -- LinkedIn wants reflective text, TikTok wants energy, Instagram wants aesthetics, YouTube wants depth
- A 40-year-old woman is in a different mindset on Facebook vs Pinterest
- "The market is the judge" -- not 4 executives in a room deciding what's "on brand"

## Clouds and Dirt

- **Clouds:** Macro vision, philosophy, north star ("I want to buy the Jets")
- **Dirt:** Micro execution, tactics, unglamorous work ("Which hashtag for this post?")
- **Ignore the middle:** Corporate politics, overthinking, meetings about meetings
- You need BOTH extremes simultaneously

## Content Model (Reverse Pyramid)

1 pillar content (keynote/podcast/vlog) > 30+ micro content pieces > distributed NATIVELY per platform > community insights > new micro content (cycle)

## Key Patterns

- "Ideas are shit. Execution is the game."
- "Macro patience, micro speed."
- "Document, don't create."
- "One is greater than zero."
