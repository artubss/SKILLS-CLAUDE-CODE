# George Loewenstein — Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/George Loewenstein.md

## Information Gap Theory

Curiosity = awareness of a gap between what you KNOW and what you WANT to know. This gap creates a cognitive "itch" that only resolves when filled.

```
Curiosity = Awareness of Gap x Desire to Fill It
```

If the viewer doesn't know a gap exists: zero curiosity.
If the viewer knows but doesn't care: zero curiosity.
Both must exist simultaneously.

## 65/35 Rule (Ideal Revelation Ratio)

- **0-50% revealed:** frustration (gap too abstract)
- **50-70% revealed:** MAXIMUM CURIOSITY ZONE
- **70-90% revealed:** tension reducing (payoff approaching)
- **100% revealed:** curiosity = zero (but satisfaction = high)

Application: Hook reveals 65% (show effect, hide cause). Development keeps 65-80%. Climax reveals 90%. Conclusion reveals 100% + opens new gap.

## Nesting Gaps (Minimum 3 Stacked)

Never open just 1 gap. Open gaps within gaps — when the viewer resolves one, they're already inside the next.

```
Gap 1 (Hook): "Why did the biggest company in the sector collapse?"
  -> Gap 2 (Context): "Who was responsible for the decision?"
    -> Gap 3 (Twist): "What exactly did they do wrong?"
      -> Gap 4 (Lesson): "How did this change the entire market?"
```

**Rule:** Gap 2 must open BEFORE Gap 1 closes. The viewer is never without a reason to stay.

## 5 Types of Information Gap

1. **Factual** — "What was the exact mistake?"
2. **Causal** — Viewer sees effect but not cause: "Why did this happen?"
3. **Procedural** — Knows result but not process: "How did they do it?"
4. **Predictive** — Doesn't know what will happen: "Will she accept or refuse?"
5. **Identity** — "What does this say about ME? Would I do the same?"

Identity Gap is the most powerful for sharing — use at the TWIST.

## Key Patterns

- "Curiosity arises when attention becomes focused on a gap in one's knowledge."
- Partial knowledge creates MORE curiosity than total ignorance (Paradox of Partial Knowledge)
- Context blocks are not filler — they transform the viewer into someone who KNOWS A LITTLE and therefore WANTS TO KNOW MORE
- Curiosity is emotionally painful. The viewer who doesn't know the ending experiences literal discomfort.
