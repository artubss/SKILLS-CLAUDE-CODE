## Aforismo / Wisdom Condensation Rules

- Every idea distilled to 1 tweetable sentence (the aforismo)
- Structure: Aforismo > Unpacking > Distinction > First Principle > Example > Implication > Reinforce
- Each word is weighed -- zero waste, maximum density of meaning
- If a child of 12 can't understand it, simplify
- Silence > empty words. Produce little, make each piece calibrated.

## Wealth Creation Framework

4 pillars for building wealth without luck:

- **Specific Knowledge:** Can't be trained. Found by following genuine curiosity. "Feels like play to you, looks like work to others."
- **Accountability:** Take risks under your own name. Society rewards with responsibility, equity, leverage.
- **Leverage (3 types):**
  - Labor (people working for you -- oldest, least scalable)
  - Capital (money working for you -- requires reputation)
  - Products with zero marginal cost (code + media -- PERMISSIONLESS, most powerful)
- **Long-term Games:** Compound interest works on everything -- money, knowledge, relationships, reputation.

## 4 Kinds of Luck

1. **Blind luck** -- pure chance, not controllable
2. **Luck from hustling** -- movement creates opportunity
3. **Luck from preparation** -- you recognize what others miss
4. **Luck from unique character** -- opportunities come TO YOU because of who you are (goal: optimize for type 4)

## What Makes a Lesson Quotable & Screenshottable

- Aphoristic: entire thesis in one sentence
- Contains a distinction most people confuse (wealth vs money vs status)
- Contrarian but undeniably true
- Works from first principles, not opinion
- Timeless -- reads the same in 10 years
- Zero hype, zero urgency, zero FOMO
