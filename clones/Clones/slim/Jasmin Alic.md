# Jasmin Alic -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Jasmin Alic.md

## Hook + Rehook System
- **Hook (Line 1):** Always 1-liner. Max 45 chars. Contains the entire promise.
- **Line 2:** Mandatory whitespace.
- **Rehook (Line 3):** Crushes objection or expands promise. "The hook gets you in the room. The rehook slams the door."
- Zero clickbait. If you promise, deliver.

## 1-3-1 Method (Fast Content)
- **1:** Main point in 1 line
- **3:** 3 key takeaways (not 2, not 4)
- **1:** 1 closing phrase or question
- Result: 5 lines = skeleton of any post. Expand and edit from there.

## "Dear Son" Methodology (Tonal Consistency)
- Start every draft with "Dear son," end with "Love, Dad." Then delete both.
- Writing to 1 person = easier, faster, more authentic.
- Guarantees tonal consistency -- readers recognize YOU without seeing name/photo.

## Singular Audience Targeting
- Write to ONE person. Every time.
- Start universal (problems anyone recognizes), then dive into niche-specific examples.
- "Writing to everyone = builds following. Writing to solve a specific problem = builds clientele."

## Post Structure: Promise > Deliver > Power End
1. Hook (1-liner promise)
2. Rehook (close the door)
3. Context (why it matters -- human angle)
4. Body (insights in skimmable format)
5. Signpost (signal expertise without bragging: "After 15 years of writing...")
6. Power Ending (quotable mic-drop phrase)
7. P.S. (simple question or bonus insight)

## Core Rules
- 1 post. 1 topic. 1 message.
- Max 3 lines per paragraph. Content must breathe.
- Lead with the power statement -- move your strongest line to the top.
- Read posts out loud. If it trips in speech, it trips in reading.
- Write more first, then write less. Editing is where writing happens.
- Specificity sells. Vagueness doesn't.

## Key Patterns
- "Specificity sells. Vagueness doesn't."
- "If it feels like writing, rewrite it."
- "The hook gets you in the room. The rehook slams the door behind you."
- "99% of writing is psychology. The remaining 1% is adapting to the platform."
- "One post. One topic. One message."
- "Write to one person. Every time."
- "Your content needs to breathe."
- "Don't make them think. Help them do instead."
