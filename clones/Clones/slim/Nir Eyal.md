# Nir Eyal — Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Nir Eyal.md

## Hook Model (Habit-Forming Cycle)

```
TRIGGER --> ACTION --> VARIABLE REWARD --> INVESTMENT
   ^                                          |
   +------------------------------------------+
```

- **Trigger** — What initiates the behavior (thumbnail + caption)
- **Action** — Minimum behavior required (click + watch first 3s)
- **Variable Reward** — Unpredictable payoff that creates compulsion
- **Investment** — What the user puts in (time watched, comment, save)

## Variable Reveal (Never Close Without Opening)

**Core rule:** Never reveal everything at once. Always reveal just enough to create expectation of what's next.

```
BAD (predictable): "The company lost R$2B in 2008 and never recovered."
GOOD (variable): "The company lost R$2B in 2008. But the number nobody talks about is what happened in the next 6 months."
```

Never close a block without opening the next gap.

## 3 Types of Variable Reward

1. **Rewards of the Tribe (Social)** — Validation, belonging, "I understand something others don't" = share trigger
2. **Rewards of the Hunt (Information)** — Valuable insight, the exact data point the viewer wanted = save trigger
3. **Rewards of the Self (Completion)** — Mastery, growth, "this video was worth my time" = satisfaction

The LESSON block should deliver all 3 simultaneously.

## Investment Principle

- 0-5s: investment = 0 (can leave without cost)
- 30s watched: low investment (slight psychological cost to leave)
- 90s watched: medium ("I've watched almost everything, might as well finish")
- 150s watched: high ("finished, worth saving/commenting")

**The 90-Second Rule:** If the viewer reaches 90s, probability of finishing increases dramatically. First 90s build investment, not just entertainment.

## Key Patterns

- Predictable rewards create weak habits. VARIABLE rewards create compulsion.
- The brain releases more dopamine in ANTICIPATION of reward than in the reward itself.
- Structure is predictable (7 blocks), content is variable (data, twists, scale).
- "Habits are formed by the uncertainty of rewards, not rewards alone."
