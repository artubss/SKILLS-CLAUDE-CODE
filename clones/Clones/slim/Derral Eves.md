## YouTube Ranking Formula (3 Primary Metrics)

In order of importance:

1. **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** — % who clicked after seeing thumbnail+title
   - Min: 4% | Good: 6-8% | Excellent: > 10%
   - Controlled by: packaging (thumbnail + title)

2. **AVD (Average View Duration)** — Average time watched
   - 40-50% of video = excellent for long-form
   - Controlled by: content quality, narrative structure, hook

3. **Session Watch Time** — Total watch time the video generates in the viewer's session
   - Best video = the one that makes the viewer keep watching OTHER videos on the channel
   - Controlled by: end cards, playlists, end screens

## Browse Features / Suggested Video Optimization

| Source | Typical % | What Optimizes It |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Suggested** (60-80%) | Primary | High retention + CTR — algorithm recommends to similar viewers |
| **Search** (10-20%) | Discovery | Natural keywords in title + clear thumbnail |
| **Browse** (10-15%) | Subscribers | Consistent publishing + channel affinity |

**Focus on Suggested** — create videos that retain AND chain into sessions.

## Audience Retention Graph Diagnostics

| Pattern | Diagnosis |
|---------|-----------|
| Strong drop at 30s | Hook didn't confirm title promise |
| Drop at min 2-3 | Context too long before real hook |
| Drop at min 8-10 | Climax disappointing or missing re-hook |
| Gradual constant drop | Content lacks tension / no open loops |
| Peak and rise at end | Strong CTA / memorable ending |

## Click-to-Watch Funnel

Optimize bottom-up: first retention, then CTR.

```
1,000 impressions --> CTR 6% --> 60 clicks --> 45% AVD --> 27 watched half --> 10 watched another video
```

## Key Patterns

- "YouTube is not a social platform. It's a search and recommendation engine."
- "CTR gets them in. AVD keeps them. Session time rewards you."
- "If your retention graph has a cliff at 2 minutes, your hook lied."
- "Don't optimize for views. Optimize for what happens after the view."
