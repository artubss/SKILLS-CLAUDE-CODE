## The 5 Stages of Awareness

Audience awareness level determines EVERYTHING: angle, hook, depth.

| Stage | What They Know | Approach |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| **1. Unaware** | Don't know they have the problem | Start with story/curiosity — never the problem directly |
| **2. Problem Aware** | Know the pain, not the cause | Hook = name the problem surprisingly |
| **3. Solution Aware** | Know solutions exist, not which | Hook = reveal the right solution (and why others fail) |
| **4. Product Aware** | Know options, haven't chosen | Hook = differentiate — why THIS is superior |
| **5. Most Aware** | Already a fan/follower | Hook = novelty, exclusivity, deeper insight |

**Critical rule:** Speaking at the wrong awareness level = zero engagement regardless of quality.

## Calibrating Messaging Per Level

- **Unaware** — More initial context needed before conflict. Lead with curiosity.
- **Problem Aware** — Hook enters the mental conversation already happening
- **Solution Aware** — Go straight to the paradox/surprise
- **Product/Most Aware** — Speed to conflict, depth on differentiation

## Mass Desire vs. Product Desire

- **Mass Desire:** What the audience already wants (status, money, security, belonging)
- **Product Desire:** What the content offers
- The creator's job is to connect the two: "Join the conversation in your prospect's mind"

## Breakthrough Headline (3 Components)

1. **Reach** — How many in the target audience will be touched?
2. **Intensity** — How strong is the desire the headline activates?
3. **Credibility** — Does the viewer believe the promise is real?

## Key Patterns

- "You cannot create desire. You can only channel existing desire onto your product."
- "Find out what your prospect is already thinking — then join that conversation."
- "The headline is the ad for the ad."
