# Sahil Bloom -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Sahil Bloom.md

## Viral Thread Structure
- Hook: curiosity, surprise, or bold statement in 1-2 lines
- Quick context: why it matters (use "starter sentence" for uncommon concepts)
- Framework or numbered list: clear, scannable, memorable
- Each point = self-contained micro-unit (works as standalone tweet)
- Concrete examples or data as proof
- Reframe or paradox that shifts perspective
- Memorable closing principle
- Soft CTA: share, save, follow, newsletter

## Hook Formulas
- **"N + Concept + Promise":** "[N] [razors/models/lessons] that will change how you [think/decide/live]:"
- **"Most People + Truth":** "Most people [wrong belief]. Here's the truth: [contrarian insight]."
- **"I Used To + Transformation":** "I used to [old behavior]. Then [discovery]. Now [result]."
- **"Harsh Truth":** "Harsh truth: [direct statement]. (But here's the good news: [solution].)"
- **"Explain Complex Simple":** "[Complex concept] explained simply: [analogy in 1-2 lines]."

## Framework-Driven Posts
- 1 post/thread = 1 central idea, never mix concepts
- Draft in bullet points first (80%+ of the way there)
- Polish: transform bullets into short punchy prose, eliminate fluff ruthlessly
- When in doubt, replace fluff words with data
- Vocabulary: 5th-grade level. If a child can't understand, rewrite.

## Newsletter Growth Mechanics (The Curiosity Chronicle)
- **Content Engine:** 1hr daily reading > Notion idea board > pick what magnetizes > draft in bullets > polish > test on social > recycle winners
- **Frequency:** 4x/week (Mon: short actionable, Wed: deep dive, Fri: "Friday Five" curated, Sat: week-in-review)
- **Funnel:** Social media > Newsletter > Book > Speaking > Investments
- **Tone:** Write every issue like sending to your closest friend
- **Lead magnets:** Annual Planning Guide (50K+ downloads)

## Power Business Writing Rules
- Punch the reader in the face with your first sentence
- Short, punchy writing is better. Space sentences out.
- Be ruthless eliminating fluff. Replace fluff with data.
- Test the hook before writing the full piece (tweet first, thread later)

## Key Patterns
- "Most people overestimate what they can do in a day and underestimate what they can do in a year."
- "Curiosity is the fountain of youth."
- "Never let the quest for more distract you from the beauty of enough."
- "Small actions compound into big results."
- "The good old days are happening right now."
- "When in doubt, zoom out."
- "Harsh truth: Money is a terrible barometer of happiness."
- "Bookmark this. You'll need it."
