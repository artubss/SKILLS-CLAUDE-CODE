# Robert Cialdini — Core Frameworks (Slim)

## 7 Principles of Persuasion

1. **Reciprocity** — Give value first, create psychological obligation to return. Deliver genuine insight before CTA.
2. **Commitment/Consistency** — Once committed, people act consistently. 90s watched = committed; abandoning feels incoherent.
3. **Social Proof** — In uncertainty, people follow others. Use scale data or collective references ("most people believe...").
4. **Authority** — People follow experts. Numeric specificity is the #1 authority signal in short video. "R$4.3B in 8 months" > "made a lot of money."
5. **Liking** — Influenced by those they like. Similarity + genuine identification creates affection.
6. **Scarcity** — What's rare is more valuable. Loss possibility > gain possibility. CTA that requires action = perceived scarcity.
7. **Unity** (most powerful) — People follow those who are PART OF THE SAME GROUP. "We who [shared characteristic]..." Identity, not similarity.

## Pre-Suasion (PRINCIPIO_DOMINANTE)

The mental state BEFORE the message determines up to 60% of its effectiveness.

**3 Priming Channels (CANAL_PRIME):**

| Channel | Activates When | Content Must Confirm With |
|---------|---------------|--------------------------|
| **Personal Relevance** | Viewer thinking about themselves | Hook using "you" or direct question |
| **Trust** | Viewer in confident state | First data point with immediate numeric specificity |
| **Curiosity/Openness** | Viewer in open cognitive state | Hook with paradox or expectation inversion |

**Sequence:** Thumbnail activates Channel 1+3 --> Caption activates Channel 2+3 --> First 1.7s confirms the prime --> Hook closes the loop.

**Critical rule:** If thumbnail/caption prime and hook are incongruent, viewer enters distrust (not productive confusion) and leaves.

## Unity Principle (Applied)

| Mechanism | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| Shared identity | "We who [common trait]..." |
| Implicit tribe | "Those who understand this are the same who..." |
| Co-discovery | "When I saw this number, I thought the same as you..." |

## Key Patterns

- "Pre-suasion is getting people into the right frame of mind before delivering your message."
- "Unity is not about similarity. It is about shared identity."
- "What we attend to before a message determines how we process the message."
- Reciprocity before CTA: value first, ask second.
