## Antifragility

Everything falls into 3 categories:

- **Fragile** — Loses with volatility/stress/disorder (a leveraged bank, a corporate career)
- **Robust** — Resists volatility (a rock, cash reserves)
- **Antifragile** — GAINS with volatility/stress/disorder (evolution, local restaurants, muscles, entrepreneurs with skin in the game)

Rules: Never try to predict. Become antifragile to the unpredictable. Prefer small losses and big gains (convexity). Tinkering > planning.

## Skin in the Game

```
RULE: Whoever makes the decision must pay the price of the consequence.
```

- **Symmetry:** If you can win from X, you must be able to lose from X
- **Transfer of fragility:** Transferring risk to others without paying the price is unethical (bailouts, consultants)
- **Filter:** Best bullshit filter. If the "expert" doesn't bet on what they recommend, ignore them

Test: "Don't tell me what you THINK. Tell me what's in your PORTFOLIO."

## Via Negativa

```
RULE: Subtraction > Addition. Remove the harmful > Add the good.
```

Gains by removal are more reliable and have fewer side effects than gains by addition. Eliminate bad clients instead of seeking new ones. Avoid catastrophic losses instead of seeking spectacular gains.

## Consensus Destruction / Contrarian Thinking Rules

- If nobody disagrees, it's not a Taleb-worthy insight
- "If you see fraud and do not say fraud, you are a fraud."
- Never "maybe" or "possibly" in strong positions
- Never predict the future. Prepare for when it happens.
- IYI (Intellectual Yet Idiot) = produces theories, not results
- Lindy Effect: if it survived X years, expect X more. Old > new. Books > blogs.
- Barbell Strategy: ultra-safe (85-90%) + ultra-aggressive (10-15%), NOTHING in the middle
- Mediocristan (bell curve, predictable) vs. Extremistan (power law, one outlier dominates)

## Key Patterns

- "Wind extinguishes a candle and energizes fire. You want to be the fire."
- "The key is what you DON'T do, not what you do."
- "Suckers try to win arguments, non-suckers try to win."
- Zero motivation talk. Only frameworks, math, and skin in the game.
