# Lara Acosta -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Lara Acosta.md

## SLAY Framework (Post Structure)
- **S -- Story:** Open with a personal story (makes content unique and relatable)
- **L -- Lesson:** Extract the lesson (why it matters to the audience)
- **A -- Actionable Advice:** Provide practical, implementable tips
- **Y -- You:** Close connecting back to the reader (question or CTA)

## 6-Part Post Structure (Advanced)
1. **Hook** -- Grab attention (8-10 words, works like a YouTube thumbnail)
2. **Re-hook** -- Add curiosity (second layer, keeps the reader)
3. **Lead** -- Tell reader what they'll learn (contextualizes)
4. **Body** -- Deliver the hook's promise with real value
5. **Power-ending** -- 2-line impact summary
6. **CTA or CTE** -- Clear action or engagement invite

## Educational Storyteller Method
- Combine 3 elements per post: **Platitude** (broad relatable statement) + **Story** (personal experience) + **Lesson** (practical takeaway)
- Transform "How to" into "How I" -- personal experience > generic knowledge

## Open Loop Technique
- Every line functions as an individual hook
- Each sentence creates an unanswered question resolved only in the next line
- "Treat every line you write as a hook."

## Storytelling Authenticity Rules
- Personal branding is NOT oversharing -- it's strategic storytelling
- Every personal story MUST have a business lesson
- "Don't create content. Document your journey."
- Vulnerability is strategic, never gratuitous
- Rule of One: 1 problem, 1 solution, 1 thing per post

## Content Pillars
- **Educate:** Teach a specific skill (builds credibility)
- **Inspire:** Share journeys and transformations (builds connection)
- **Entertain:** Light content, humor, behind-the-scenes (builds community)
- **Promote:** Social proof, cases, results (builds authority)

## Key Patterns
- "Someone less qualified than you is doing what you want to do because they took action."
- "Content is king, but relatability is queen."
- "Stop trying to find a niche. Become THE niche."
- "Good content is 20% writing and 80% psychology."
- "Write like you think, not like you talk."

- "That's literally it."
