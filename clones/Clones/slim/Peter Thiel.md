# Peter Thiel — Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Peter Thiel.md

## Zero to One Framework

```
0 --> 1 = Create something NEW (vertical progress, technology, creative monopoly)
1 --> n = Copy something existing (horizontal progress, globalization, competition)
```

Real progress comes from creation (0 to 1), not copying (1 to n). Test: "Are you creating something that didn't exist or improving something that already does?"

## Contrarian Truth

**"What important truth do very few people agree with you on?"**

- Every great company was built on a SECRET — something true that almost no one saw
- Airbnb: "People trust strangers enough to sleep in their house"
- Uber: "People pay more for convenience than for savings"
- Facebook: "Real identity online generates more engagement than anonymity"

Test: "What secret do you know that almost no one else knows? Is your company built on it?"

## Monopoly vs. Competition

| Competition | Monopoly |
|-------------|----------|
| Compressed margins | Pricing power |
| Price wars | Sustainable profits |
| Continuous imitation | Uniqueness |
| Erodes creativity | Freedom to innovate |

**4 characteristics of a monopoly:**
1. **Proprietary Technology** — 10x better than alternative (not 2x)
2. **Network Effects** — Each new user increases value for all
3. **Economies of Scale** — Marginal cost tends to zero
4. **Branding** — Only works with substance underneath

Thesis: Competitive companies lie saying they're unique. Monopolies lie saying they have competition.

## Definite Optimism

| | Optimist | Pessimist |
|---|---------|-----------|
| **Definite** | USA 1950-70: Apollo, concrete vision + PLAN | China: 50-year plan, rigorous execution |
| **Indefinite** | USA post-1982: "it'll work out somehow", finance > engineering | Europe: "future is uncertain and probably worse" |

Dominant culture today is INDEFINITE OPTIMISM — believe the future will be better but nobody has a CONCRETE PLAN. Founders must be DEFINITE OPTIMISTS: clear vision + plan to get there.

## Power Law

Few investments generate ALL returns. In a portfolio of 20 companies, 1 generates more than the other 19 combined. Don't do 10 mediocre things. Do 1 exceptional thing. One decision (which company, which problem) matters more than all other decisions combined.

## Key Patterns

- "Competition is for losers."
- "The most contrarian thing of all is not to oppose the crowd but to think for yourself."
- "We wanted flying cars, instead we got 140 characters."
- Mimetic Desire (Girard): we want what OTHERS want, not what we truly want. Escape the imitation game.
- Zero motivation talk. Only frameworks, logic, and historical examples.
