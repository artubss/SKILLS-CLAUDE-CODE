# Nicolas Cole -- Core Frameworks (Slim)

> Excerpt for pipeline context injection. Full: Clones/Nicolas Cole.md

## Rate of Revelation
- The speed at which you reveal new information to the reader.
- On the internet, high speed wins. Every sentence must add something new.
- If the reader can skip a sentence and miss nothing, delete it.
- Eliminate unnecessary transitions ("That said...", "Furthermore...")
- Never open with "In this article I will discuss..." -- death to Rate of Revelation.
- 1 new idea per sentence. Short paragraphs (1-3 lines max).

## 1/3/1 Structure (Opening Formula)
- **1 sentence:** Strong opener (hook)
- **3 sentences:** Clarify, expand, add credibility
- **1 sentence:** Transition or promise of what's coming next

## The Promise (Headline Formula)
Every headline must contain 3 pieces of information:
1. **What it's about** (topic)
2. **Who it's for** (implicit or explicit audience)
3. **What problem it solves / what it promises** (benefit)
- The headline does 80% of the work. If it doesn't promise something specific, nobody reads the rest.

## 4A Framework (Writing Angles)
1. **Actionable:** "How to [X] in [N] steps" -- practical instruction
2. **Analytical:** "Here's why [X]% of [group] fail at [Y]" -- data and analysis
3. **Aspirational:** "I quit my job and [result]" -- inspiration
4. **Anthropological:** "Here's why [human behavior] happens" -- cultural observation
- 1 topic x 4 angles = 4 content pieces (idea multiplier)

## Formatting for Digital Consumption
- Max 3 lines per paragraph, ideally 1
- Aggressive whitespace and spacing
- Numbered lists and bold headings for scannability
- "Every. Single. Sentence. Must. Earn. Its. Place."
- First sentence sells the second. Second sells the third. Reader decides sentence by sentence.

## Article/Post Structure
1. Headline with The Promise (what + who + benefit)
2. 1/3/1 opening (hook, context, transition)
3. Numbered list or Main Point structure
4. High Rate of Revelation throughout
5. Concrete example per point (personal or known case)
6. Short, actionable conclusion (1-2 sentences)

## Key Patterns
- "This isn't creative writing. This is digital writing. Different rules."
- "If the reader can skip a sentence and miss nothing, delete it."
- "The difference between amateurs and professionals is [insight]."
- "Most people think [X]. They're wrong."
- "Notice what I did there?"
- "Start writing. Today."
- "100M+ views across platforms. Here's what actually works."
- "That's it. That's the framework. Now go use it."
