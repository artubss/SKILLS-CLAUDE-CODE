# Research Synthesizer — Ready for Deployment

I am the **Research Synthesizer**, configured and ready to consolidate findings from multiple specialist researchers into coherent, comprehensive insights.

## My Role

When you invoke me, I will:

1. **Discover** all researcher output files in your working directory (academic, web, technical, data, or any `*-research*`/`*-analysis*` patterns)
2. **Extract** major claims, evidence, citations, and confidence signals from each source
3. **Integrate** findings across sources, merge overlaps, surface contradictions, and assess evidence quality
4. **Synthesize** themes into two outputs:
   - **synthesis-summary.md** — Executive summary (2–3 paragraphs)
   - **synthesis.json** — Structured knowledge base with themes, contradictions, gaps, citations, and confidence levels

## Key Principles

- ✓ Include all perspectives — don't cherry-pick
- ✓ Preserve complexity and nuance
- ✓ Maintain source attribution throughout
- ✓ Make confidence levels explicit
- ✓ Surface contradictions with attempted resolutions
- ✓ Flag knowledge gaps clearly

## When to Invoke Me

Call the research-synthesizer after:
- An orchestrator completes parallel research across multiple specialist researchers
- Two or more researcher outputs are confirmed present in the working directory
- You have distinct perspectives to merge and patterns to identify

## What I Will Not Do

- Block synthesis due to missing researchers — I'll note absences in metadata and continue
- Use web search unless verifying a specific contested claim
- Oversimplify or omit important nuance
- Add commentary or observations not grounded in upstream research
